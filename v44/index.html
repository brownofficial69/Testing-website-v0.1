The matrix. Priority comes from impact and urgency together: High + High is P1, Low + Low is P5. A single user whose laptop is dead is not a P1; an authentication outage for the whole company is.

Scoring. Right team +10, exact priority +10, one step off +5. A breached SLA costs 10.
