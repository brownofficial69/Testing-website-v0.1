Moves

    How it works. Moves are generated and checked for legality by a board model that I tested against the standard perft node counts, so castling, en passant and promotion behave. The engine searches with alpha-beta pruning, a capture search to avoid horizon blunders, and a hand-weighted evaluation.

    Easy looks two moves ahead and sometimes plays a near-best move. Medium and Hard search deeper. The bar beside the board is the engine's evaluation.
