Six ways to explore the same CV, and three games

Each one turns my work into something you can pull, play or fly through. They run in your browser and use only what is on my CV.

Threat Graph

orbit · search · correlate

Every project, skill and framework as a linked node in 3D space. Search it, filter it, fly into a node, or press Correlate and watch it cluster the way ThreatWeave groups indicators.

Intrusion Replay

tune rules · stop the attack

The SOC Detection Lab as a game. Watch a four-stage intrusion with every rule off, then tune the detections: too loose floods the analyst, too lax and the data leaves.

CyberCity

fly · tour · click a tower

My six projects as landmarks in a 3D night city, with traffic, rain and a guided tour that flies into each one.

Three science games

Small experiments from the maths and the history behind security. Each one lets you find the result yourself.

Cipher Lab

frequency analysis · index of coincidence

Break a Caesar shift, a substitution cipher and a Vigenère cipher using the methods cryptanalysts discovered, from a ninth-century manuscript to the 1920s.

Play →

Worm Outbreak

epidemic model · network hubs

Contain a worm with 10 patches using the SIR epidemic model. Patch at random and it spreads; patch the hubs and the threshold collapses.

Play →

Collision Lab

birthday paradox · hash collisions

Guess how many hashes it takes to find a collision, then watch the maths win. A hash breaks at about the square root of its size, not its size.

Play →
